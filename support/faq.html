Questions before the first order.

Everything that is already clear—and the information we will publish before taking payment.

When will orders open?

We will open orders only after each product has its final price, MRP, complete label information, shipping terms, returns process and customer-care details.

What is currently on the MarwarMade shelf?

Red Chilli Powder, Turmeric Powder and Coriander Powder are listed in 200 g, 500 g and 1 kg packs. The Rajasthan range includes Mathania Red Chilli in a 200 g whole-chilli pack, Dried Ker and Dried Sangri.

How should I use and store the spices?

Each product page has use and storage guidance. In general, keep pouches sealed in a cool, dry place and use a clean, dry spoon.

Will prices and delivery estimates be shown before payment?

Yes. Price, applicable delivery charges, availability and final order information must be visible before checkout is enabled.

Are Ker Sangri and its ingredients available separately?

Ker and Sangri are now shown as separate Rajasthani ingredients. Ker Sangri is the preparation made by bringing the two together; final pack, label and ordering details for both ingredients will be published before sales begin.

How can I contact MarwarMade?

Our customer-care email, WhatsApp number and business address will be published on the contact page before sales start.
